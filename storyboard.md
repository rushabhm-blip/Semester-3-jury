# Jury storyboard — draft 1

**How it moves:** one continuous canvas. Every screen fills the screen; there are no grids of cards.
The next screen is usually hidden *inside* the current one: we zoom into a dot, a number, a sketch panel or a diagram node, and it opens into the next screen.
Between projects we pull back out to the board and fly to the next one.

`→` marks the transition into the next screen.
